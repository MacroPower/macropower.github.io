The site itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Some posts embed
players from YouTube and Spotify. Each player loads from its service,
which can set cookies of its own.

The home terminal keeps your command history in your browser's local
storage, so the history survives a reload. Clear the site's data in your
browser to remove it. The site itself stores nothing else.
