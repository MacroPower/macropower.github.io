{{- /* Renders assets/unity/icons/.svg as an of a published, fingerprinted copy, for the full-color art that the launcher, the Dash, the HUD, and the notifications repeat on every page. The browser then caches each icon once instead of reading it again in every page's HTML, and each copy keeps its gradient ids to itself. An icon that paints with currentColor renders inline through unity/icon.html instead, because an image cannot inherit the page's color. Context: the icon's name, such as "launcher/home". */ -}} {{- $src := partialCached "unity/ui/icon-source.html" . . -}} {{- if $src.currentColor -}} {{- partial "unity/icon.html" . -}} {{- else -}} {{- $res := resources.FromString (printf "unity/icons/%s.svg" .) $src.file | fingerprint -}} {{- end -}}
