# Solitaire

Klondike, the one everyone knows. Build the four foundations up from the
aces by suit; build the columns down in alternating colors. Kings alone
may start an empty column.

Drag a card or a run to move it, or click a card to send it to the first
place it fits. Click the stock to draw. Keys: N deals a new game, Z
undoes, H shows a hint, Space draws, A finishes a won game, M toggles
sound.

Nothing is saved. Reload and you get a fresh deal.
